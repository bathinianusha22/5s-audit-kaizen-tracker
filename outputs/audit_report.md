## Recommendations

1. Focus the next audit cycle on Warehouse & Stores: it is the only zone below 3.0, weakest on Standardize and Sustain. Pair a supervisor gemba walk with the open Standardize kaizen actions.
2. Protect the gains on Line 1 - Assembly: its improvement followed implemented kaizen actions. Keep the weekly huddle and re-audit the new shadow boards before the next cycle.
3. Sustain is the weakest pillar program-wide. Make the weekly 5S huddle and the audit-finding tracker standard work in every zone, not just the improving ones.
4. Convert the 8 approved ideas into implementation plans with owners and due dates before logging new suggestions, so the log does not grow faster than the team can implement.
